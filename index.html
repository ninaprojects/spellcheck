Spell Check

a month of practical magic

31 days, four witches.

We're not trying to become new people, we're just finally letting ourselves pay attention, out loud, together. Call it manifesting, but make it accountability. Call it girl math, but for the soul. Whatever you call it, it's four of us deciding to actually do the things we keep saying we'll do, and letting the sky hold us to it.

So we'll journal when we don't feel like it. Move our bodies because it feels good, not because we have to. Spend a little less. Touch some grass before we touch our phones. And talk to each other through all of it: the wins, the spirals, the 2am realizations, all of it. Nothing here is that deep and also all of it is that deep.

By the time the New Moon in Libra rolls around, we want receipts. Proof we actually did the thing. The sky's just here to keep us honest, and honestly, she's better at it than any app.

This is Spell Check. Consider it your monthly reminder to check in on your own spells.
